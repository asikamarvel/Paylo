Calculation Logic: This explains how payroll is calculated for each staff member.

Hours Classification

For each timesheet entry, the system checks the project name against the Projects configuration.
Billable Hours = Sum of hours on projects marked as "billable"
Non-Billable Hours = Sum of hours on projects marked as "non_billable"
Local Hours = Hours on projects with geography = "local"
Regional Hours = Hours on projects with geography = "regional"

Extra Billable Hours Calculation

Each cadre has a base_billable_hours threshold (e.g., 20 hours for Principals)
Extra Billable Hours = Max(0, Total Billable Hours - Base Billable Hours)
Only extra billable hours are paid at hourly rates (hours within threshold are covered by base salary)

Compensation Calculation

Base Salary = Fixed monthly amount based on cadre (NGN for local, USD for regional)
Extra Local Fees = Extra Local Billable Hours x Local Hourly Rate
Extra Regional Fees = Extra Regional Billable Hours x Regional Hourly Rate
Gross Compensation = Base Salary + Extra Local Fees + Extra Regional Fees

Deductions

Withholding Tax (WHT) = Gross Compensation x WHT Rate
Local contractors: 5% WHT | Regional contractors: 10% WHT
HMO = Fixed monthly amount per staff (configured in staff registry)
Net Pay = Gross Compensation - WHT - HMO - Other Deductions

Special Cases

Principals: ALL billable hours are paid (not just extra), because they bring in the projects
Flexible Contracts: No base salary, paid purely on hourly basis
Fixed Contracts: Fixed monthly amount regardless of hours
Advisors: Paid per hour for all billable hours (no threshold)

Configuration changes are saved to YAML files in config/ folder
